# Swedeline privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

Swedeline does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no
server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only things it stores
are your settings and two counters for you, described below, and they stay
in your browser.

## What Swedeline does on your device

- **Page text:** to replace words, Swedeline reads the text of the web pages you
  visit. This happens entirely inside your browser. The text is never sent
  anywhere and is not saved.
- **Dictionaries:** the translation word lists ship inside the extension and
  are loaded from the extension package. Swedeline makes no network requests.
- **Settings:** your preferences (on/off, amount of words, sites you turned
  Swedeline off for) are saved with the browser's extension storage
  (`storage.sync`). If you are signed in to Chrome or Firefox with sync
  enabled, your browser may sync these settings between your devices under
  its own privacy policy. Swedeline itself never sees or receives them.
- **Your counters:** the popup shows how many Swedish words Swedeline has
  shown you and on how many pages. These are two numbers, saved only on this
  device (`storage.local`). They hold no addresses, page content or words,
  are not synced and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Swedeline deletes
  them. At a few milestones the popup asks whether you'd like to rate
  Swedeline in the store; your answer is saved the same way, so it isn't asked
  again too soon. The store page only opens if you press the button.
- **Pronunciation:** the 🔊 button uses your browser's built-in
  speech synthesis. Swedeline prefers a Swedish voice that runs on your device.
  If none is installed, it uses a Swedish voice your browser provides
  online (for example Chrome's "Google svenska" or Edge's Microsoft online
  voices). Then, only when you press 🔊, your browser sends that one Swedish
  word to its voice provider, under the browser's privacy policy. Nothing
  is sent until you press the button, and Swedeline never sends page text.

## Permissions

- **Access to all websites:** needed so Swedeline can find and replace words in
  the text of the pages you read. No page content leaves your browser.
- **Storage:** to remember your settings and your counters.

## Third parties

Swedeline includes no third-party code that talks to the network and shares no
data with third parties. The only exception is the online voice described
under Pronunciation, which your browser provides and you start yourself.
